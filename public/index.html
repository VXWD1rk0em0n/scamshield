    About your privacy and these results

    Screenshots are read in your browser; only the extracted text is sent for analysis. Messages are not stored by this demo. If AI analysis is enabled, personal details are masked before the model sees the text. Trusted senders you choose to save stay in this browser.

    This is a prototype tested on synthetic messages. A low score does not guarantee a message is safe. Verify uncertain requests through a contact method you already trust.
