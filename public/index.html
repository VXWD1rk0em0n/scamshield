    How it works

    • 20 transparent rules look for the tricks scammers use: requests for codes or passwords, gift-card and crypto payments, lookalike links (chase-secure-verify.com), impersonation, pressure and secrecy, hidden characters, and text aimed at fooling AI filters.
    • Every warning points at the exact words that triggered it, and the score explains itself.
    • Personal details (phone numbers, emails, card numbers, one-time codes) are masked before any AI model sees the text. Links are never opened.
    • This public demo stores nothing. It is a prototype tested on synthetic messages - a low score is not a guarantee. When in doubt, contact the company using a number you already have.
